# Handmade work and public life

`alexandria-lives-9` is the current **free-play** birth revision: `alexandria-lives-8` with births spread from 400 to 300 BCE (`src/vocations/birth-years.ts`). The world starts in 400 BCE, so events and rules open from the start reach the earliest births, while dated history keeps its year; each birth year is weighted by how much dated history falls between ages 10 and 60 of that life, so births around 355 BCE are the most common. `alexandria-lives-8` is the retained figure-path revision: the life-stage rules of `alexandria-lives-7`, built from the retained `alexandria-demo-2` pack, with the [historical figure paths](#historical-title-integration) added. It retains deterministic household resource rolls, market-valued wealth and the connected 74-node attribute atlas (Physique, Handmade, Knowledge and Trade, with a small military lane) with recorded work proofs and proof from everyday work (see `src/capabilities/work-proof.ts`, from `alexandria-lives-7`), lets a child trade at the grain market from age 10 instead of 12 (`demo.market.minTradeAge`), and includes [life stages](../life-stages/README.md): every event has a time frame, and floods, levies, illness and town offers reach a child through the household's decisions instead of the child's. It also includes [life paths](../paths/README.md): districts whose people set pay and whom you meet, affinity toward a field, city and royal births, and a record of each life stage. The previous `alexandria-lives-1`, `alexandria-lives-2`, `alexandria-lives-3`, original `alexandria-lives-4`, `alexandria-lives-5`, `alexandria-lives-6` and `alexandria-lives-7` revisions remain available for exact replay. Started lives keep their exact pack and versioned capability rules. Ranked and practice packs remain separate; testnet configuration and deployment tooling are explicit rather than changes to an existing free-play save.

Run `node scripts/vocations/build-world.mjs` to reproduce the shipped JSON. It writes the rules-only `alexandria-lives-7` (`buildLifeStageWorld`, version `LIVES_RULES_WORLD_VERSION`) and then `alexandria-lives-8` (`buildFigurePathWorld(rules, LIVES_WORLD_VERSION)`); both versions are declared in `ids.ts`, and `alexandria-lives-7` must stay byte-identical. The builder reads the public [skill-tree design](../../design/skill-tree/README.md) for recipe inputs and reference prices.

The runtime supports:

- Thirteen recipes for thread, linen, clothes, ceramics and sculpture, with purchased or household-produced inputs, workshop fees, graded products and finite buyers.
- Learned methods control batch size, quality caps, input savings, controlled firing and paid inspection. Thread yields four units per recipe unit. Large reliefs and monuments reserve all inputs and fees at the start, pause between active work years, and produce once after two work actions.
- One shared flax/linen store with the existing farm and town market. Other materials and graded finished products live in the vocation inventory.
- Paid textile, pottery and sculpture apprenticeships; deliberate practice; philosophical study, observations, tests, independent checking and original inquiry; public addresses, negotiated agreements, account reconciliation and policy review.
- Ordinary work gains of +3 primary / +2 to each named support; deliberate practice +5 primary. Main Knowledge and Bonds effects are authored per activity. Workshop safety, research credit and public-account decisions affect Health, Knowledge and Bonds.
- Supported artisan, research, civic, trading and farming careers, including mastery appointments. Appointments require proficiency and recorded completed work. Their job definitions grant no automatic wage or skill gain: actual paid work and product sales provide income.
- Bounded material stock, lifetime buyer demand and separate merchant, workshop, school and council cash accounts. Purchases, fees, wages and sales transfer money between those accounts and the household. They never create money or borrow automatically.
- Work consumes the player's annual action and farm labour. Existing automatic job gains are skipped in a year spent on vocation work. Family helpers continue contributing to the farm.
- Deterministic commands, immutable snapshots, journal receipts, validated pack references, save/load and replay. The existing Career screen exposes work, price/skill previews, inventory and project status.

The **versioned capability evaluator** requires parent capabilities, proficiency and distinct accepted work receipts. Farming proofs wait for the actual harvest and food settlement. Artisan assessments reserve materials, fees and funded buyer payment once, then count active work years. Civic and inquiry records retain their costs and results. The two-year commercial assessment reserves two orders and delivers to two separate funded buyer accounts. No skill score or map visit grants a node by itself.

The current graph uses Physique, Handmade, Knowledge and Trade. Two Knowledge crossovers feed one military lane. Strength and Endurance are trainable; Health stays a current-condition gate. Soldier, Officer and General appointments require the corresponding capabilities and service deeds, and all three ranks use the real army wage/ration accounts. See [military crossover rules](../../guide/SKILL_CROSSOVERS.md).

Open `/skills` for the complete read-only atlas or **Skill tree** in a life to inspect prerequisites, proof progress, work quotes, rewards and career offers. See [the player guide](../../guide/SKILL_TREE.md) for controls and the focused-play evaluator. All eight mastery paths have accepted-command, save/load and replay coverage in an authored fixture that isolates proofs from survival and training balance. The **45–65%** birth-cohort mastery rate is an authored balance target rather than a guarantee; the measured 100-life cohort (`skill-focus-0` … `skill-focus-99`, `alexandria-lives-8`, `fnv1a64-ae23b3454c2d9e4d`, recorded at `3e0fc88`) spans **55.0%–64.0%** across all eight paths in [mastery-cohort-evidence.json](../../design/skill-tree/mastery-cohort-evidence.json) alongside low-wealth vs. ordinary start and resource bottleneck diagnostics. Numerical stocks, budgets and progression remain authored gameplay values.

Existing worlds keep their original engine behavior. This revision introduces no NFT mint rule or on-chain resource inventory.

## Historical title integration

New births use `alexandria-lives-8`: eleven figure-specific routes on the `alexandria-lives-7` rules (proof from work, market trade from age 10). Numeric capability, military, district, NPC and birth-era behavior is preserved. Qualified figure projects use the existing work ledger and actual material/payer balances, and yearly practice and work years still prove skills alongside them. `node scripts/vocations/build-world.mjs` builds it. The retained `alexandria-lives-6` is the same figure routes on the unchanged `alexandria-lives-5`; `node scripts/figures/build-world.mjs` reproduces it and `alexandria-figures-1` (from `alexandria-deep-1`) byte-identically. Official worlds 1–5 and 7 remain unchanged; independent proof-tree packs are `alexandria-deep-1` and `alexandria-figures-1`.
