# Historical figure paths · alexandria-lives-8

The new free-play revision connects all eleven agreed figures to the existing skill tree, authored work, replay-recorded achievements and earned special titles. Earlier free-play and ranked packs retain their original content. These paths do not alter deployed NFT contracts or the four legacy mintable figure IDs.

## Contribution and title rules

Each route has three association ranks: **Uncommon** for one completed contribution year, **Rare** for two further contribution years, and **Epic** for three further years of substantial work. Each stage requires its own relevant skills or learned capabilities before the work can be performed. Access to a famous person, attending a lesson or reaching a high score awards none of these ranks by itself.

The principal figure's rank is separate from the player's association rank. A Legendary principal does not turn an assistant into a Legendary contributor. The principal's figure-linked special title requires a separate two-year capstone and assessment. The special title describes the legacy represented by the completed route; it does not assert that the player's character was the historical person.

Preparatory work opens from age 12 and develops the relevant existing proficiencies. Direct access opens from age 18 within the authored historical window. Entry and travel consume a work year and an actual fee. Later schools, workshops or surviving accounts provide a distinct tradition route after most direct windows close. Tradition titles do not claim a posthumous personal meeting or endorsement. Euclid's entry is expressly a geometrical teaching circle; an individual meeting is uncertain.

The Demetrius entry is an authored service encounter after the Gaza campaign, not a documented personal meeting. Its earlier start is necessary to fit the required engineering and project years before the Rhodes branch closes. Later tradition access can still support association titles; a missed Rhodes work window cannot manufacture the timed campaign capstone.

## Resource and evidence model

Figure work uses the existing annual-action and vocation ledgers. It gives the existing work gains: +3 primary proficiency and +2 to each of up to two supports. Fees transfer to the workshop; wages debit finite school, workshop or council accounts. Paid materials come from the existing suppliers. Inputs are removed from actual inventory and shared household stores.

The supply paths consume 20, 40 and 60 grain per contribution year and 80 per capstone year, in addition to writing or workshop materials. Pytheas's provision and observation work also consumes grain and linen. Lysippan work consumes actual bronze and fuel. Drama consumes papyrus, ink and rehearsal linen. The value of a completed assignment is not inferred from a paper purchase alone.

Claim activities consume no second annual work year; they certify already completed and suitably qualified work. They grant no cash or skill-point currency. Awards retain their source activity and outcome in the standard accomplishment record. Progress rejects mismatched sources, invented outcomes, future records and achievements outside an entry window.

The revision also adds **22 once-only development decisions**, two per figure. They concern record keeping, collaboration, revision and credit, and give only small relevant skill or stat effects. Their appearance is not required to unlock a route or complete its work.

## Conditional Legendary outcomes

Demetrius, Ptolemy and Zeno each have a separate, one-shot success/failure resolution. Attempt records block rerolling; failed outcomes preserve the earlier Epic work without awarding the promotion achievement. Dates alone cannot promote a figure.

- **Demetrius:** `rhodes-captured` is expressly alternate history.
- **Ptolemy:** `ptolemaic-dynasty-founded` requires the funded founding programme and a successful independence/succession result, not centuries of later history.
- **Zeno:** `stoic-school-established` requires a sustained functioning school. This remains the working interpretation of “could push Legend.”

Alexander also has a one-shot decisive-operation resolution. Losing that operation earns no Legendary figure title, even with excellent scores and earlier contribution ranks.

The 65-node tree is retained, with one local revision repair: P3 accepts a recorded comparison that finds either agreement or disagreement. Matching actual records is valid inquiry; an early household with identical bid/ask prices must not invent a discrepancy to learn comparison. The frozen lives-3 rule is unchanged.

## Evidence boundaries and validation

Dates, locations, biographies and work types are supported by the sources carried in each figure's metadata and the biography catalogue. Exact project costs, player encounters, rank names, ceremonies and alternate outcomes are authored game design. Titles are not claims that historical people conferred these exact honours.

Mechanical reachability, save/load/replay, resource conservation and failed-outcome checks are tested separately from normal-life balance. High-proficiency, generous-resource fixtures can establish that a path is mechanically reachable; they do not estimate how often an ordinarily focused life earns its final title.


## Verification

All eleven complete figure routes are exercised through real engine commands. Fifty-two focused checks cover chronology, actual skills and capability proofs, material and payer balances, title provenance, direct/circle/tradition wording, all successful and failed conditional promotions, Alexander's decisive campaign failure, one-shot restrictions, the interface, saved accomplishments and replay. The complete TypeScript check passes.

Mechanics fixtures deliberately author high proficiency, health and resources; early Alexander and Aristotle entry also has moderate-proficiency checks. These establish reachability and correctness, not an ordinary-life success rate. Representative balance simulations remain separate. Retained free-play and ranked packs keep their existing rules and deployed NFT IDs.

Final repository regression result: **1,565 passing / 1,584 total**, with the **same 19 failing tests as the pre-figure-path baseline**. No additional failures were introduced. The remaining baseline failures cover existing content-version, birth-resource/economy and legacy UI/event expectations. Strict TypeScript and diff checks pass.

## Integration with current main

The combined default is `alexandria-lives-8`, built from the `alexandria-lives-7` rules (proof from work, market trade from age 10) by `node scripts/vocations/build-world.mjs`. The retained `alexandria-lives-6` carries the same paths on the unchanged official `alexandria-lives-5`. It retains the attribute atlas, military lane, life stages, district paths and NPC systems while adding the eleven historical paths. Official worlds 1–5 remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The independent 65-node proof engine is preserved under `src/deep-capabilities`, using `alexandria-deep-1` and `alexandria-figures-1`; it does not replace the official numeric capability engine.
