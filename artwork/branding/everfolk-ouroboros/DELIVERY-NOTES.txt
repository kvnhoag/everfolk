EVERFOLK / OUROBOROS IDENTITY
11 November 2026

CONCEPT
An original serpent bites its own tapered tail, closing a circular form.
The open centre and continuous body express the repeated lives and stories
behind Everfolk. Endless Life is the working name of the mark; Everfolk
remains the game name and Live another story remains its motto.

REFERENCE
The official Assassin's Creed title and emblem assets were visually studied
for sharp silhouette, high contrast, generous negative space and restrained
uppercase title treatment. The Everfolk serpent and title outlines are
original geometry. No Assassin's Creed artwork is included in this pack.
https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/assassins-creed

EDITABLE MASTER
masters/Everfolk-Ouroboros.vectorcraft contains eight transparent artboards:
01 Primary ink signature, 1200 x 800
02 Primary reverse signature, 1200 x 800
03 Primary heritage gold signature, 1200 x 800
04 Horizontal signature, 1200 x 300
05 Ouroboros integrated as the O, 1200 x 300
06 Separate wordmark, 1200 x 260
07 Detailed serpent emblem, 512 x 512
08 Compact serpent emblem, 256 x 256

The serpent is native filled vector geometry, with a true transparent
centre, mouth opening and eye slit. The head and body were united in
VectorCraft to remove a shared-edge rendering seam. The compact version
omits the eye. Title letters are individually editable vector outlines;
supporting motto text remains live in the master.

VERIFICATION
The native master was saved, closed and reopened with all eight artboards.
The reopened emblem was rendered and visually checked. The primary proof,
identity sheet, integrated O and compact icon were reviewed. No export
warnings or supporting-font substitutions were reported. SVGs contain
vector geometry without embedded raster images.
