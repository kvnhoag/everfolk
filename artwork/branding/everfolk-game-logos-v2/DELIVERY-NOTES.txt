EVERFOLK / GAME TITLE LOGOS / REVISION 2
11 November 2026

Direction selected by the user: historical strategy, with bold lettering,
engraved metal and stronger emblems. This describes the visual direction.

Twenty new title compositions were created in ArtCraft VectorCraft using
native editable paths, customised outlined lettering, metallic gradients,
vector extrusion and bevels. Bronze, copper and silver finishes extend
Everfolk's deep teal and antique gold branding.

INDIVIDUAL LOGOS
masters/ : 20 editable VectorCraft documents.
Each master has two artboards:
  1. Transparent logo, 1000 x 560.
  2. Logo on a dark teal proof background, 1000 x 560.
Letter shapes, metal treatments, emblems and ornaments remain editable.
The title lettering is outlined geometry; it can be edited as paths.

svg/ : 20 transparent SVG logos with outlined lettering and vector finishes.
png/ : 20 transparent PNGs at 2000 x 1120, plus 20 dark proofs at 1500 x 840.
No fonts are required to display the SVG or PNG exports.
Dark fills within plaques and emblems are part of the metal design.

VALIDATION
All 20 native masters were saved, closed and reopened in VectorCraft.
Each retained its two artboards and editable artwork. The set was rendered
and visually reviewed, including corrected motto and ornament collisions.
SVG exports contain vector art and outlined lettering without raster images.
No export warnings or font substitutions were reported.
The logos have not been applied to the game.
