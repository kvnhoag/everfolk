# Everfolk — Dawnwing crown refinement

The head is now a **sharp three-point crown**, with a taller central peak, two pointed side peaks and a solid flat base. The smooth wing roots, body, legs and inner channels are carried forward from the previous refinement.

Open `review/Dawnwing-Crown-Comparison.png` to compare the previous soft head with the crown. `review/Dawnwing-Identity-Proof.png` shows the crown version as a standalone emblem, with the approved Eczar wordmark and at small sizes. Matching editable VectorCraft review documents are included.

`masters/Everfolk-Dawnwing-Crown.vectorcraft` contains eight transparent artboards: standalone emblem, stacked signature, horizontal signature and independent wordmark, each in ink and cream. The eight SVG and PNG production exports are under `svg/` and `png/`; icon sizes from 16 to 256 pixels are also included. The production SVGs use outlined paths with genuine transparent negative spaces.

The master was saved, closed and reopened in VectorCraft with all eight artboards and zero raster images. Export warnings were empty. The comparison and identity proofs were rendered and visually checked.

`source/dawnwing.cjs` retains the curve constructions and crown polygon. `source/original-dawnwing.cjs` is the previous soft-head design, retained for the comparison. On this installation, run `node source/build.cjs` to rebuild using the ArtCraft MCP helper and VectorCraft CLI; the helper closes its task-owned headless process on completion.

The standalone scarab and the full Everfolk. name remain independently usable. Lettering is the existing clean outlined Printed Folktale artwork: Eczar 600, −0.025 em tracking and the clay-coloured period.
