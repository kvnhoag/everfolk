EVERFOLK / ILLUSTRATED STORY IDENTITY
11 November 2026

The user selected Pentiment / Wildermyth: illustrated, story-driven identity.
This fourth set develops twenty options in that direction, organised as
four composition families with five illustrated variants each.

PENTIMENT
The published designer case study was read and the actual logo image was
visually inspected. Its uneven display lettering, prominent initial,
printed texture, dark ink contours and small decorative accents informed
the hand-cut capitals and clipped vector grain in the Everfolk studies.
Everfolk uses an original E/V/R/F/O/L/K glyph set and its own palette.
Source:
https://www.skylightcollective.com/case-study/xbox-pentiment/
Official game page:
https://www.obsidian.net/games/pentiment

WILDERMYTH
The actual standalone logo on the official website was visually inspected.
An illustrated initial, irregular storybook letterforms and a long curling
letter stroke informed the integration of pictures into the Everfolk E,
and the botanical, feather and travel flourishes extending from the title.
Everfolk uses original letters, illustrations and flourish geometry.
Sources:
https://wildermyth.com/
https://wildermyth.com/img/wildermyth1_logo.png
https://wildermyth.com/press/sheet.php?p=wildermyth

EVERFOLK'S OWN STORIES
The pictures represent a traveller beginning a path, people meeting,
a scribe at work, a traveller at another shore and a growing legacy.
These reflect Everfolk's personal stories and choices across historical
worlds. They are symbolic illustrations, not archaeological reconstructions.

01-05 / Illuminated initial: a narrative picture sits inside the E.
06-10 / Printed tale: cut capitals, vector grain and a story at the initial.
11-15 / Lettering in bloom: illustrated strokes grow from the title.
16-20 / Living chronicle: a small life vignette accompanies drawn lettering.

Only original Everfolk artwork is included in the logo files. Reference
logos are identified here by their official sources.
