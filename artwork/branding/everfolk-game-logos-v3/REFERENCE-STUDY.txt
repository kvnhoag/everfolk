EVERFOLK / GAME LOGO REFERENCE STUDY
11 November 2026

This revision starts from visual inspection of published game logos on
official publisher and developer pages. The title lettering was then drawn
as original vector paths for Everfolk. Reference artwork is not included
in the Everfolk logo files.

01-04 / INSCRIPTION
References: Civilization VII and Anno 1800.
Observed principles: widely spaced capitals, strong contrast between fine
and broad strokes, restrained ornament and an open title silhouette.
Everfolk response: Horizon Inscription, a new set of E/V/R/F/O/L/K paths,
with wedge terminals, tapered strokes, a long R leg and a clear oval O.
Official sources:
https://newsroom.2k.com/resources/sid-meier-s-civilization-vii-logo-2-2
https://www.anno-union.com/games/anno-1800-lead-the-industrial-revolution/
https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/anno/1800

05-08 / CALLIGRAPHY
Reference: Crusader Kings III.
Observed principles: character comes from calligraphic cuts, angular
terminals and a distinctive word shape; a flat pale treatment can work.
Everfolk response: Chisel Uncial, eight original capital/lowercase glyphs
with asymmetric bowls, flared ascenders, an open E and angled crossbars.
Official source:
https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/crusader-kings-iii/about

09-12 / GEOMETRY
Reference: Manor Lords.
Observed principles: restrained geometric capitals, compact stacked words,
flat contrast and a simple divider create a clear title silhouette.
Everfolk response: Threshold Capitals, seven original capitals with
clipped corners, diagonal V/K rhythm and transparent polygonal counters.
Official source:
https://manorlords.com/

13-16 / LIVING LETTERS
Reference: Old World.
Observed principles: organic lowercase lettering and a human, living
character can distinguish a historical title from a monumental inscription.
Everfolk response: Living Stone, seven new lowercase forms with broad
curves, swept feet, flared terminals and slight asymmetry.
Official sources:
https://www.oldworldgame.com/
https://mohawkgames.com/

17-20 / DISPLAY TITLE
Reference: Age of Empires IV.
Observed principles: scale relationships, nested lines and tall proportions
produce an identifiable title silhouette with restrained supporting rules.
Everfolk response: large and small custom capitals, nested EVER/FOLK and
one hybrid capital/lowercase signature. Everfolk has no added sequel number.
Official source:
https://www.ageofempires.com/games/age-of-empires-iv

The comparison board labels these references so the design rationale can
be assessed alongside the twenty Everfolk options. The directions reflect
the user's historical strategy visual preference and preserve the brand
name and motto, Live another story.
