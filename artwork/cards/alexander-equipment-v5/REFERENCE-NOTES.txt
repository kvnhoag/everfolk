EVERFOLK / ALEXANDER / EQUIPMENT REVISION V5

Purpose
Extend the actual-reference workflow from face/hair to sword, armour and dress.
Retain the v4 imagined face, readable gaze, mounted gesture and painterly style.

Actual visual references
1. Greek iron machaira/kopis, 5th-4th century BCE, former Met object 2001.346,
56.5 cm overall, 0.7 kg. Official public-domain photograph shows two weapons;
the UPPER weapon guides the new curved blade profile and compact hooked grip.
https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/257576
https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/api/collection/v1/iiif/257576/1752237/main-image
Local: references/greek-machaira-met-2001.346.jpg
The record says the object was restituted in September 2025 and is no longer
in the Met collection. It is a sword-type reference, not Alexander's weapon.

2. Alexander Mosaic, MANN inv. 10020, House of the Faun, Pompeii.
Late second century BCE, inspired by a pictorial model.
https://www.museoarcheologiconapoli.it/mosaici/
https://www.museoarcheologiconapoli.it/portfolio-item/mosaici/
Official photo: MANN / Giorgio Albano.
https://www.museoarcheologiconapoli.it/mann/uploads/2025/07/1-Battaglia-di-Dario-e-Alessandro-inv.-10020.-ALBANO-scaled-1.jpg
Local: references/mann-alexander-mosaic-10020.jpg
Detail: references/mann-alexander-armour-detail.png, a faithful ArtCraft view
crop of the original photograph, not a generated reconstruction. Source JPEG
remains unchanged. It guides shoulder pieces, edged cuirass, chest device,
waist ties and closely spaced overlapping skirt strips (pteruges).
The museum page does not establish the cuirass's material. Surface, material,
hidden construction and details not legible in the mosaic are reconstructions.

3. Mounted Alexander bronze, MANN inv. 4996, Getty conservation publication.
First century BCE; potentially a Roman adaptation of a Hellenistic prototype,
with restorations. The article identifies a silver-inlaid rosette fastening
the mantle on the rider's RIGHT shoulder. Use its drape and fastening only;
do not mistake the horse's larger bridle rosettes for the rider's clasp.
https://www.getty.edu/publications/artistryinbronze/conservation-and-analysis/44-siano/
https://www.getty.edu/publications/artistryinbronze/assets/img/figures/fig_44.01a_Siano_Melillo.jpg
Photo: Salvatore Siano, by permission of MANN.
Local: references/naples-alexander-mounted-cloak.jpg
The extra oblique front photograph in this folder is supporting context only.

Production
Built-in image editing uses the v4 artwork as the edit target and the actual
references above as equipment guides. Exact prompt: source/generation-prompt.txt.
The generated source is source/alexander-equipment-revised.png. It is not
an authenticated reconstruction of Alexander's complete personal panoply.
Sword grip material, clothing colours and hidden construction are artistic
choices. No new individually authored brush pass is claimed for v5.
PixelOE 1.0.0 uses a 420 x 525 logical grid enlarged exactly 2x to 840 x 1050.
ArtCraft keeps frame, text, rarity, emblem and footer independently editable;
the character illustration itself is flattened. Final pixel PNG is composited
at 100% under native frame/type to avoid raster interpolation.

Reference photographs stay in the review/source workflow, separate from the
generated card illustration. Prior v3/v4 variants remain unchanged. Card rarity,
flavour and numbering are exploratory; no NFT is minted.
