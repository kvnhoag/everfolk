Calibration
Keep the exact existing 3-pixel coarse blocks outside the head selection.
Use a 420 x 525 / 2x PixelOE contrast detail pass for the face, with outline
expansion thickness 0. This avoids merging small eyelid/pupil structures into
heavy dark marks. Pure area downsampling and a 60%-area/40%-contrast mixture
were inspected and rejected because they muted iris highlights.
No pupil, iris or catchlight is newly drawn. Existing source detail is sampled
on the finer grid; the source's chiaroscuro shadow remains.

Regional composition

in source/face-detail-config.json, specifically for this approved composition.
Coarse and fine grids share origin (0,0). Whole 6 x 6 cells, the least common
multiple of 3 and 2, choose either complete coarse or complete fine pixels.
The outer ring uses a fixed Bayer threshold to choose whole cells, with no
colour/alpha blending or boundary blur. This is a mixed-grid finish, not a
single uniform logical image. 603 common cells select detail (21,708 pixels),
with 21,485 changed pixels. Every unselected pixel matches the old coarse base.
Both configured eye rectangles are covered entirely by the detail pass.
