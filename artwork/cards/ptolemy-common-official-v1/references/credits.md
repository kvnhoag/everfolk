# Junior Court Official references

The two generated paintings edit the project's existing Ptolemy Common painterly illustrations, retaining their character identities, linen/ochre clothing, courtyard setting and brushwork. The new seated desk action, background supervisor and fictional rank are creative game direction, not a reconstruction of a named ancient official.

- **Writing instrument and tablet construction:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art, *Bronze stylus*, Cypriot, Classical or Hellenistic, 4th–1st century BCE, object 74.51.5495. The museum identifies a pointed writing end and flattened erasing end and describes recessed wooden wax tablets. Public-domain photograph saved unchanged as `met-bronze-stylus.jpg`. The generated tool follows the pointed writing function; its decorative terminal is an interpretation, not an exact object copy. [Object record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/244374), [original photograph](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/api/collection/v1/iiif/244374/499314/main-image).
- **Administrative task:** Museo Egizio, *Greek Petition*, Cat.2153, Ptolemy IX's first reign. Its petition and administrative response support the general paperwork theme. This is later Ptolemaic evidence, not a document from Ptolemy I's reign. [Catalogue](https://collezionepapiri.museoegizio.it/en-GB/document/583).
- **Seated writing activity:** Acropolis Museum, *Statue of a scribe*, Ακρ.144, 510–500 BCE. Used as textual evidence for a seated writer with tablets and stylus. This Archaic sculpture predates the game setting and is not a costume reference. The museum photograph could not be retrieved because its site requested a browser challenge; no imitation reference was generated. [Object record](https://www.theacropolismuseum.gr/en/statue-scribe).

Both final inner illustrations use the established PixelOE finish: a 280×350 logical coarse pass enlarged to 840×1050, with a registered 420×525 face-detail pass selected in complete cells. QA verifies determinism, original source preservation and zero changes outside the face-detail selection. Frames and typography are rebuilt separately from editable sources and preserve the approved subtitle spacing.
