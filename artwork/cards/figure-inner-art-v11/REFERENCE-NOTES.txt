EVERFOLK FIGURE INNER ART V11 — 13 NOVEMBER 2026

User direction
Use the supplied character's own armour reference for Alexander. Do not borrow
another historical person's armour. Use the second supplied equipment sheet
for simpler military contribution equipment. Extend inner art to the full
collection and apply the established pixel filter.

Scope
13 named parent figures and 35 earned contribution/title subcard illustrations.
The default current figure paths contain 11 figures with three contribution
stages each. Nearchus and Eumenes are also included from the playable classic/
sample title paths. This is an art library; the application's authored events,
awards, ownership, drop rates and display components are not changed by it.
Title aliases in catalog.json link the eight classic earned titles to suitable
artwork; they do not add recognition or mutate a saved player's identity.

Source authority
src/figures/world.ts defines the 11 core figure paths, their work and stage titles.
research/figure-biographies.json documents the figures and evidence boundaries.
scripts/alexandria-historical-titles.mjs and worlds/alexandria-sample.json define
the additional Nearchus and Eumenes title paths.

User-supplied images
references/user-alexander-character-armour.png is the definitive main-character
outfit design reference, copied unchanged from the user's attachment. The body
armour, edging, Gorgon motif, sash, scales, skirt and forearm equipment inform
the painting. The bare-headed face and longer corrected sword are retained from
the mounted composition anchor. The helmet is not imported into this pose.
references/user-military-equipment-sheet.png is the user's simpler equipment
reference for military service subcards. It remains unchanged. Neither source
has been presented as an authenticated archaeological photograph, and no
external provenance/artist attribution was supplied with these attachments.
They are labelled as user-supplied design references in the review gallery.

Interpretation
Other named figures have imagined appearances and role-appropriate clothing
informed by the local authored content. No exact ancient likeness or personal
panoply is authenticated here. The same Greek armour is not applied to scholars,
artisans, navigators or administrators. Subcards depict representative unnamed
player contributors performing the authored tasks. Their scenes do not establish
historical personal pupil relationships or undocumented real meetings. Tools,
architecture, gestures and lighting are artistic reconstructions.

Production
Built-in image generation creates the painterly illustrations. Exact prompts,
source PNGs and individually selected face-detail regions are retained. No API
CLI fallback, editable ArtCraft brushwork or independently rigged clothing is
claimed. All paintings are flattened illustration assets; previous versions
and the ordinary portrait library remain unchanged.
references/prior-mounted-edit-target-v10.png preserves the exact previous
painting used as Alexander's edit target. Its old armour is superseded; its
composition, identity and corrected sword are the retained elements. The new
source/alexander-parent.png then serves as a style anchor for the other scenes.

Pixel finish
The bundled Everfolk pixelate.py applies PixelOE 1.0.0 legacy CPU to an exact
280 x 350 logical coarse grid, enlarged 3x to 840 x 1050. pixelate_regions.py
then selects whole registered cells from a 420 x 525 finer pass for the face,
using the approved 3px-body / 2px-face method. This is a mixed-grid output.
It does not smooth, sharpen or regenerate eyes. Each actual composition has its
own explicit face ellipse. Repeat-render determinism, source preservation,
integer-grid integrity, lossless PNG readback and zero changes outside the
regional selection mask are checked by the scripts and recorded in qa/.
source/build-catalog.mjs independently rechecks source/final hashes and required
PixelOE flags before recording an asset complete.

Review and delivery
review/index.html contains the complete filterable gallery, per-image painting
and pixel comparison, exact prompts and the supplied reference images.
catalog.json records all assets, title aliases, hashes, dimensions and scope.
The review sheets are resized presentation summaries; full source and filtered
PNGs are retained separately. See per-worker manifests for visual review notes.
