Requested correction
The previous weapon looked oddly angled. Its handle rose steeply while the
proximal blade turned into a shallow sweep immediately beyond the guard.
The selected revision lowers the fist, bends the forearm naturally and raises
the entire weapon on a coherent diagonal. Handle/tang/proximal blade share a
continuous direction. The forward curve develops farther along the blade.
Heroic blade scale and the flying cape are retained as artistic choices.
