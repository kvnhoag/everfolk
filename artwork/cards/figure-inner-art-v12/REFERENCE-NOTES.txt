User corrections
Correct Ptolemy's confusing main-card arm routing. Every subcard role must cover
both male and female contributors. Vary face angles for all subcard sets, not

Scope
13 main historical figures, plus 35 player roles with two gender variants each:
70 subcards and 83 total artwork files. 11 core figures have three contribution
stages; Nearchus and Eumenes each have a playable classic/sample title role.
The named figures remain themselves; male/female variant labels refer to the
representative player contributor, not alternate genders of those figures.

Pose and angle revision
Ptolemy's right arm on image-left now descends clearly from its own shoulder
and rests on the document stack. His left arm on image-right holds his own
scroll near the waist. The ambiguous across-body hand-to-hand exchange is
removed. Face, diadem, clothes, background and painterly character remain.
The old painting is retained as references/ptolemy-anatomy-edit-target.png.

Uncommon contribution scenes use frontal head views, Rare scenes use left-
facing profiles, and Epic scenes use three-quarter views. Existing original-
gender Epic scenes are retained where suitable, except Alexander's full set
is revised. Nearchus female title uses a profile view and Eumenes female title
a frontal view. The two retained male title scenes keep their existing views.
Face-angle intent is a visual design contract, not an automated anatomical test.

Artwork sources
24 unchanged v11 assets are copied with original image bytes and original QA,
as recorded in reuse-manifest.json. Old absolute paths within those original
QA records are provenance; copied source, coarse, final and config hashes are
independently rechecked in the new catalogue. 59 new paintings/edits use the
built-in image generator. Exact prompts and original generated PNGs are saved.
Input scene/identity references from v11 remain preserved in that library.

The user-supplied Alexander character armour and military equipment sheet are
copied unchanged in references/. The Alexander source painting is a style
anchor, not another person's costume to be transferred. No other named ruler's
armour is researched or borrowed. Other appearances and role settings remain
imaginative, not authenticated portraits or archaeological reconstructions.

Finish and validation
All selected paintings use the approved PixelOE 1.0.0 CPU finish: 280 x 350 coarse
logical grid enlarged 3x to 840 x 1050, with whole-cell regional selection from a
420 x 525 finer pass for individually selected face regions. This is a mixed
3px-body/2px-face grid, without smoothing or generated eye details. Source,
coarse, final, config and QA hashes; repeated-render determinism; PNG readback;
whole-cell integrity; and zero outside-mask changes are checked. Originals,
per-image QA and stage/gender metadata are retained. No application code or
gameplay state is changed. User acceptance of the new art is not assumed.
