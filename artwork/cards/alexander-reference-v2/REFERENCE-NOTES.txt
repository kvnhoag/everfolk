REFERENCE STUDY
1. Naples, Museo Archeologico Nazionale, inv.4996, Alexander on Horseback.
   Getty conservation publication:
   https://www.getty.edu/publications/artistryinbronze/conservation-and-analysis/44-siano/
   Image credit: Salvatore Siano, by permission of MANN. The source is used
   to study the rider's three-quarter torso, raised sword arm, low rein hand
   and energetic horse. It is not pasted into the generated card. The object's
   restorations and attribution have scholarly qualifications; this card is
   an original imagined illustration, not an archaeological reconstruction.
2. Taddeo Zuccaro, Alexander the Great and Bucephalus, c.1553, NGA1991.182.1.
   https://www.nga.gov/artworks/73841-alexander-great-and-bucephalus
   NGA labels the media public domain. Used for the rider/horse diagonal,
   gesture and movement, not as evidence of ancient costume.
3. Warner Bros. official production-book excerpt for Alexander(2004), chapter1.
   https://pdl.warnerbros.com/wbol/uk/movies/alexander/downloads/book/chapter_one.pdf
   Printed page3: Alexander reviews the phalanx at Gaugamela. Used for the
   mounted commander, horse foreground, receding pike ranks and sense of scale.
   The actor's likeness and exact photographic composition are not reproduced.
   The generated dawn lighting is an authored Chiaroscuro reinterpretation.
4. The previous hybrid anime/Chiaroscuro portrait supplies the working imagined
   identity and rendering language, while the new pose and setting replace the
   former seated harbour scene.
