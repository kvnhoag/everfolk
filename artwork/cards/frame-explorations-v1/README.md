# Everfolk — five additional card frame directions

Generated with the built-in image generator on 13 November 2026. Every board pairs a standard inset-art frame with a legendary full-art treatment. The ochre fields are empty artwork placeholders, not finished card illustrations. These are raster design concepts for selection; they are not editable production frame assets.

1. **Scribe's Folio** — ivory vellum, oxblood binding and botanical ink details.
2. **Civic Monument** — pale stone, bronze inlay and architectural framing.
3. **Living Mosaic** — jade and terracotta tesserae with a deliberate geometric border.
4. **Woven Heirloom** — indigo cloth, ivory stitching and clay embroidery.
5. **Night Archive** — minimal dark jade, fine silver rules and a nearly borderless legendary treatment.

The legendary layouts all keep artwork continuous to the trim edges; titles and footer float over the image. No combat rules or additional rarity hierarchy are established by this exploration.
