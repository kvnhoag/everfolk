# Everfolk ordinary portrait library v1

**Superseded for final portrait use by [ordinary-v2](../ordinary-v2/README.md).** V2 adds shared physical M/F bodies, fitted clothing, per-age skin matches and the agreed coarse/fine finish. These v1 original head sources, provenance, content mappings and pixel engine remain required dependencies; preserve them.

Player, ordinary NPC and relationship portraits. Created 13 November 2026. Historical figures, titles and TCG artwork belong to the separate card workflow.

Open the [offline portrait gallery](review/index.html) to browse all 192 portraits, or inspect the [lifetime sheet](review/lifetimes-6x6.png) and [wardrobe sheet](review/adult-wardrobe-6x8.png). Start agent work with [AGENT_MAP.md](AGENT_MAP.md), then [manifest.json](manifest.json) for exact generated paths and hashes. [mappings.json](mappings.json) connects the current world to wardrobe families. [source-index.json](source-index.json) locates every original, exact local prompt and provenance record. The library is staged separately; this task does not change the live portrait selector, relationship UI or scene actors.

## Production calculation

There are six shared visual identities, `m01`, `m02`, `m03`, `f01`, `f02`, `f03`, matching the current two recorded sex values and three avatar variants per sex. Each has infant, child, youth, adult, middle and elder forms: **36 independently generated head-and-neck sources**. These are six fictional identity designs, not 36 unrelated people. This initial pool can be expanded later to reduce visual repetition among NPCs.

| Clothing family | Eligible portrait stages | Composites across six identities |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Everyday household | All six | 36 |
| Work: workshop, field and domestic trades | Child, youth, adult, middle, elder | 30 |
| Maritime: harbor, fishing and seafaring | Youth, adult, middle, elder | 24 |
| Commerce: shops and trade | Adult, middle, elder | 18 |
| Scholarly: writing, accounts and teaching | Youth, adult, middle, elder | 24 |
| Military service | Adult, middle, elder | 18 |
| Elite: court and estate administration | Adult, middle, elder | 18 |
| Performance | Youth, adult, middle, elder | 24 |
| **Total** | **32 family/stage pairs** | **192** |

The wardrobe has **11 generated source garments**: eight adult family designs, plus infant swaddle, child tunic and youth tunic. Stage-specific normalized garments reuse those source paintings. The work family includes children because workshop apprenticeship begins at age 12, within the current child portrait stage. The age-12 work image is an optional selection; a younger child does not acquire an occupation merely by sharing that portrait stage.

Relationship labels do not multiply this count. Parents, grandparents, spouses, children, friends, teachers, disciples, business partners and enemies use the same identity/age/clothing system. A change of relationship must preserve the person's identity. Family resemblance is not inferred or generated by this library.

## Source and finish

- Smooth head/neck and garment sources were made with the built-in image generator. Originals remain unchanged in `source/`. Exact prompts stay in ignored `source/prompts/*prompt*.txt`; per-asset provenance records point to them.
- Heads and clothing are actual separate transparent PNG components, not clothing labels inside a flattened painting. Normalized components use the shared 800 x 1000 assembly canvas. Each component's crop and placement are recorded by the builder.
- Layer order is opaque background, clothing, then head/neck. Crop bounds and placement transforms are recorded in the build metadata. The six ordinary identity designs are independent of occupation and natal household.
- The assembled portrait is flattened to an opaque source before the PixelOE CPU transformation. The chosen profile is **128 x 160 logical pixels, 3x integer enlargement to 384 x 480, outline thickness 0, source-color matching and no palette reduction**. Pixelation uses contrast-aware downsampling, with repeat-render and source-hash checks. It is not a generated pixel-art imitation.
- Review actual-size previews as well as larger contact sheets. Pixel-grid checks alone do not establish a good likeness, clothing fit or readable gaze.

## Visual references and interpretation

The agreed language is mature anime character construction, realistically structured anatomy, painterly chiaroscuro, warm light from upper left, cool jade/umber shadows, restrained earth colors and readable eyes. Clothing family colors, the ordinary people's faces and the occupational groupings are authored art direction. They are not measured historical traits or attested job uniforms. The garment layer is a shared frontal bust design, not a full-body rig or a universal reconstruction of male and female ancient dress.

Authentic public-domain museum photographs are retained unchanged in `references/` and shown beside generated examples in the review material:

Museum photographs retain their own object lighting and colors. They are evidence of forms, not evidence for the generated faces or the costume palette. Additional source links and interpretation boundaries are listed in `mappings.json`.

## Runtime handoff

The current application selects portraits by origin, recorded sex, variant and age; it does not yet select clothing by job. The manifest bridges existing slots and documents proposed selection, but it does not install a new runtime binding. Existing SVG assets and saved-life behavior are retained.

Use the recorded job or occupation to select professional clothing. A natal origin alone must not equip a child, dependent or minor royal relative with a profession or crown. Unknown occupation falls back to everyday dress. Identity must remain stable when a job changes. Age gates apply to the actual age, not only the broad portrait stage.

Relations need a separate UI/data adapter: family tables currently have no portrait cell; relatives have no persisted avatar variant; children lack recorded sex and avatar variant. Do not infer sex from a name, alter simulation RNG or invent persisted identity data while consuming these assets. The agent map describes these integration boundaries.

## Rebuild and verification

Use [scripts/build-library.py](scripts/build-library.py). It uses the existing local PixelOE runtime and installs no dependencies. Run its `--help` for the exact proof/full-build options. The completed build used `--build --logical-width 128 --scale 3 --thickness 0`. Every final has repeat-render, exact-pixel-block, PNG readback and unchanged-source checks. [scripts/index-sources.py](scripts/index-sources.py) rebuilds the source index, and [scripts/build-review.py](scripts/build-review.py) rebuilds the offline gallery and reference credits after a full build. [scripts/verify-library.py](scripts/verify-library.py) checks hashes, paths, current-world coverage and job eligibility; the delivered [audit](qa/library-audit.json) passes. The manifest is the authoritative output index; QA JSON and review sheets accompany the build. New identity or wardrobe revisions should use a new version directory and preserve v1 sources.
