FINISHING METHOD

downsampling, with restrained settings and source colour matching.
https://github.com/KohakuBlueleaf/PixelOE
PixelOE is the finishing method, not the source image generator.
Its local runtime is isolated under ignored artifacts/pixeloe-runtime; project
application dependencies and global Python environments are not modified.

Review the fine and stronger grids at actual display size, especially gaze,
brow, mouth, hair silhouette, garment folds and background vessel silhouettes.
The user's style choice remains reviewable; do not call a test approved merely
because its pixel grid is technically regular.
