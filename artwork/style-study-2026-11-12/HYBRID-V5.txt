12 November 2026

USER REQUEST
Combine the anime/cartoon visual direction with the aura of the original
Chiaroscuro portrait, retaining the separate pixel-finishing workflow.

SOURCE REDESIGN
Built-in image editing used the clean anime scene as the edit target and
the original Chiaroscuro portrait as a lighting/presence reference.
The new clean source keeps Alexander's identity cues, pose, simple historical
clothing, table and harbour composition. The lighting, shadow structure,
expression and rendering were redesigned at source rather than merely graded.

STYLE RULES FOR THIS CANDIDATE
Anime: deliberately drawn adult facial features, coherent stylized hair locks,
clear cloth shapes and selective coloured outlines.
Chiaroscuro: concentrated warm directional light; sculptural facial planes;
connected cool brown/jade shadows; edges receding into darkness; quiet dark
surroundings that leave the eyes, face and hand as the attention hierarchy.
Intended presence: contained authority, inward thought, watchfulness and a
trace of human fatigue, rather than a uniform heroic scowl.
The result intentionally allows more painterly volume than the earlier bright
cel illustration. The user has not yet approved the balance or pixel strength.
