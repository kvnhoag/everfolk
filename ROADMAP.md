# Everfolk roadmap

**Live another story.** Alexandria is the first playable world choice; future settings are independently selectable worlds.

| Phase | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Simulation foundation | Seeded rules, calendar, commands, validation and replayable saves | Implemented |
| 2. A complete human life | Work, skills, wealth, relationships, care and consequences | Implemented; balance evolves |
| 3. Alexandria | A historically grounded setting with varied beginnings and careers | Playable |
| 4. Player experience | World selection, birth, decisions, scenes, records and accessibility | Implemented; ongoing refinement |
| 5. Authoring and release | Owner content studio, immutable publication, reproducible builds and CI | Implemented |
| 6. Deeper ambitions | Sustained historical careers, scholarly work and lasting consequences | Eleven figure-specific paths implemented in new free-play lives; balance evolves |
| 7. More world choices | Another complete historical setting using the shared engine | Future; setting and order unresolved |
| 8. Optional connected services | Accounts, shared stories and community creation | Separate future decisions |

- [x] **Special-title paths mapped to the deep skill tree.** `alexandria-lives-6` adds all eleven figure routes, historically reachable encounters and later traditions, real qualified projects, Uncommon/Rare/Epic contribution titles, separate figure-defining capstones and the agreed outcome-dependent Legendary promotions. Alexander, Aristotle and Euclid require earned achievements as well. See [the route matrix](design/figure-paths.md). Normal-life balance remains unmeasured; deployed ranked/NFT minting is unchanged.

The core game comes first. Later ambitions and a second world can develop partly in parallel after their foundations are stable. Connected services are optional. This roadmap has no fixed delivery dates.
